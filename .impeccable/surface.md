# BIX dashboard
Mode: Operate. Code-led, user-pinned screenshot direction.
## Direction contract
THESIS: Distinguish free funds from total balance and broker-reported bonus progress before showing historical performance. No ornamental trading-terminal features.
OWN-WORLD: Ink navy surfaces, cyan selection and chart stroke, restrained violet secondary accents, mint gains and coral losses. Light counterpart uses cool white surfaces and dark readable teal. System UI workhorse type, tabular financial numerals, aligned compact tables.
STORY: Select account, see real balances and daily results, record a session, choose a personal date interval. Theme and installation live in persistent header controls.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Slim four-destination left navigation, account header and theme/install actions. Free balance and a precise total/bonus breakdown occupy the main reading area; cumulative bonus turnover occupies the adjacent progress rail. Historical closing chart and goals follow, then a combined journal/transaction timeline. Mobile uses bottom navigation and stacked sections. Forecast is removed.
FORM: User-pinned screenshots and confirmed dark-cyan choice; no random selection overrides the brief. Signature interaction: remembered light/dark switch transforms every surface without losing journal state.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
