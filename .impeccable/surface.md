# BIX dashboard
Mode: Operate. Code-led, user-pinned screenshot direction.
## Direction contract
THESIS: Make daily account progress and personal target periods easy to read and edit; no ornamental trading-terminal features.
OWN-WORLD: Ink navy surfaces, cyan selection and chart stroke, restrained violet secondary accents, mint gains and coral losses. Light counterpart uses cool white surfaces and dark readable teal. System UI workhorse type, tabular financial numerals, aligned compact tables.
STORY: Select account, see real balances and daily results, record a session, choose a personal date interval. Theme and installation live in persistent header controls.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Slim left navigation, account header and theme/install actions, four compact financial readings, wide equity graph beside goals, journal below. Mobile becomes bottom navigation and stacked content; all controls remain accessible.
FORM: User-pinned screenshots and confirmed dark-cyan choice; no random selection overrides the brief. Signature interaction: remembered light/dark switch transforms every surface without losing journal state.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
